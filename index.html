PRODUCT JUDGEMENT

Where I would start

01 / Isolate protocol failures

Investigate Ember’s legacy charger handshake. Inspect message traces and reproduce failures in a protocol test harness before choosing a fix.

02 / Test the consent experience

Clarify access permissions and test shorter copy. Measure completion alongside informed consent; send reminders only where permitted.

03 / Validate unmet demand

Use unsupported requests to shortlist integrations, then compare reachable demand, partner access, delivery effort and commercial value.

Metric definitions, assumptions & validation plan
  • Connection success: live-data journeys ÷ supported requests. Retried successes included. Unsupported requests are excluded, and measured through coverage.
  • Time to connect: median minutes from start to first data, among connected journeys. Synthetic medians are calculated from individual durations, not averaged manufacturer medians.
  • First-time success: journeys reaching data without a retry or support contact ÷ supported requests.
  • Device coverage: supported requests ÷ all requests. This measures observed request coverage, not share of all devices in the market.
  • Stages are cumulative. A final failure cause maps to exactly one stage and owner. Waiting/in-progress journeys are excluded from this completed cohort.
  • To validate: agree the definition and freshness threshold for “live data”, instrument journey IDs, consent events, handshake events, errors, retry counts, support contacts and timestamps. Define deduplication, observation windows, privacy and retention with engineering.
  • Next research: interview installers, support and integration engineers; validate root causes; test the proposed diagnostics and prioritisation workflow. No customer research or measured business outcomes are claimed here.
